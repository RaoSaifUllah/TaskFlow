# ADR-003: PostgreSQL durable notification queue

Status: accepted baseline, 2026-10-01.
Implementation evidence: see ../Implementation-Status.md; acceptance is not a verification claim.

## Decision
Store notification jobs in PostgreSQL in the same transaction as their originating mutations.

## Rationale and consequences
Lease claims and fencing are required. External delivery may duplicate after uncertain outcomes; exactly-once external delivery is not promised.

Source: document/02-Software-Design.md. No deviation from the supplied architecture.
